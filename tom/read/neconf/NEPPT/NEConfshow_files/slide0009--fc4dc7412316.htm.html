Advanced Language Courses
•
•The news-keeping up
•Advanced tools
•Individualized instruction
I want to shift the focus from beginning courses to more advanced work, in particular courses at the senior year level, normally following an in-country semester or year.

These students need constant contact with the high level of language they have grown accustomed to- I suggest that they all follow and monitor the news and keep abreast of sites that interest them in particular.

But it also became clear to me students wanted the ability to work at a high level of sophisticated language in  specific and individual fields of interests. My response was to prepare a course in a topic of some general interest-ecology!
