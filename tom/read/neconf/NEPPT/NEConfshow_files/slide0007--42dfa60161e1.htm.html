Begin in  reverse order and note that we should not be re-inventing the wheel. Many excellent programs exist out there, where one can learn alphabet, the sounds, phrases and grammar-some even in context. I have incorporated several of those links into my own modest contribution.

I have already referred to Hypercard program on the Russian alphabet. Several years ago I did a program called Russia Today that has one hundred signs in Cyrillic, such favorite iconic images as Coca Cola, Pepsi Cola, Pizza Hut, and the like. When one presses on the picture the word is pronounced and in addition the English translation and transliteration are shown.  Still popular site with thousands of this per year.

More recently I have developed for my own students the beginning  of a web based course or text, that proceeds from alphabet to a focus on forms approach,that  provides students with the vocabulary to answer questions who, what, where, when and why.

They are offered in a link I have provided to a series of them for you.
