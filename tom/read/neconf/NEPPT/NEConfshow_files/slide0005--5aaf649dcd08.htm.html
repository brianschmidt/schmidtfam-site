PowerPoint Presentation - Internet for Authentic Texts and Tasks

Considerations
•Timeliness of Texts
•Authentic language tasks
•Unlimited personal content
Selection  of texts and tasks should  revolve around institutions with proven staying power-the Moscow Metro and the Bolshoi Theater are likely to survive whatever changes Russian society or its representation in electronic form undergoes.

The tasks, in so far, as possible should represent true task.  To a large extent-the web is used by native speakers to locate and process information. By its very nature, most web sites serve this purpose of providing content and can be sued by students for the same reason.

Personal content has long been the most challenging choice for textbook writers. To include one or two or ten hobbies in a language text is also to exclude dozens, if not hundreds, of others.  With the ability to find information specifically related to one’s interest, students now have the opportunity to acquaint themselves with and assemble their own personalized vocabulary lists-and at a rather specific and sophisticated level.
