On a practical level, I have wanted to incorporate web materials into my basic Russian language course at Middlebury since the web’s inception.  I do distinguish between two types of sites.

There are numerous easy to use, readily available programs for learners-one of the better ones (although) not in Russian is provided on the BBC site.

Then of course there is the entire library of materials intended for the native speaker.  One might reflect that everyone now has immediate access to a reference collection and resource, electronic texts, news media-printed, audio and video-ever increasingly that means we have 24/7 access not even dreamed of a few years ago.
