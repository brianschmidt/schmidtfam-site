PowerPoint Presentation - Internet for Authentic Texts and Tasks

Challenges
•Constantly evolving
•Appropriate content
•Time on task
The constantly evolving nature of the web guarantees fresh and up-to-date material, but also insures that some links, even those of yesterday will no longer work or exist.  The exponential growth of the resources condemns to failure any attempts to stay current beyond the smallest possible range of interests.  The web is timely, but anything but timeless.

What constitutes culturally appropriate and age appropriate materials can differ and in Russian certainly does.  In one application for listing in personal ads columns my students met a student-aged sixteen, who answers yes to smoking and describes his drinking as social (only on holidays). There is also the ever present one click away from inappropriate content matter, however, you define that for your audience and your language group.
Time on task is crucial to language learning-students learn what they practice doing.  If they read more, they will better readers. But passive  or no interaction doesn’t constitute quality learning.
