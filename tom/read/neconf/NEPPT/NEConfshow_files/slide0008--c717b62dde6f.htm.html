In addition to the tools I have just described, let me give a quick two minute guide to Russian language resources on the web. The first tool I provide is a Russian English online dictionary. One of the simplest to use can be found at rambler.ru. Students can cut and paste any Russian word and get an instant translation. If they bookmark this site, they are off and running.
Other tools are search engines, rambler.ru, yandex.ru, google.ru, encyclopedias and other reference works
Russians use sites for everything from checking the weather, getting a map, sending postcards, shopping, checking the movie and theater schedule and ordering  tickets.  Students can do the same.

In addition, students can use sites intended for one specific use, for more pedagogical practice. The metro map. Can be the source of a game to move one around the city.  Students can locate places of interests on a map. They can read personal ads to develop acquire and develop their own vocabularies.

When in doubt, type in the word plus .ru  there is a Russian snowboarding and more recently Russian karate.
