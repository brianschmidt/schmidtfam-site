The Meaning of Love

Edited with a substantially revised translation by Thomas R. Beyer, Jr.

One of Solovyov's most important works.
"The meaning and worth of love as a feeling is that it really forces us, with all our being, to acknowledge for another the same absolute significance which, because of the power of egoism, we are conscious of only in our own selves."
