Getting by In Russian

Getting By In Russian: A Beginners' Course for Tourists and Businesspeople.
Book and two cassettes.

(Barron's: 1997)
ISBN 0-8120-8448-9

Ideal for someone planning a trip. Contains a brief introduction to the alphabet and how to read signs. The most important expressions will help you to get around your hotel, do some shopping, travel about the city and visit the theater.

This is the new revised edition published in January 1997.
