Russian Culture

We can speak of Culture with a capital "C", music, theater, literature, the fine arts and the history of a country. In the following pages I will try to provide some key links or connections to the capital C variant.

Culture with a small "c" is made up of those things specific to a country and its way of life. I have made copies (scanned images) of the realia that makes up Russian life. Many of the items are useful for language learners and those just plain curious about Russian life today.

The images may be copied and printed on color printers. They are intended primarily as a treasure chest or storehouse of artifacts of Russian life for use in language and culture learning settings.Click for a sample of a Russian notepad.
