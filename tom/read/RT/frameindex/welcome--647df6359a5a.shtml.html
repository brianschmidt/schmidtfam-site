Welcome to Russia Today

Welcome to Russia Today -- a look at the language and culture, people and places of that mysterious, mystical, magnificent land.

Russia Today is a work in progress. A collection of items to help you learn the language and get closer to the people and their culture. There are lots of pictures, maps, everyday items and links to interesting sites.
