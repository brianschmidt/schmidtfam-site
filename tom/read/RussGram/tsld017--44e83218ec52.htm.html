    * If stems end in consonants then spell e for the first person singular and en for the third person plural.
    Second conjugation verbs end in bnm or tnm
    Then endings will be Cnjzn (to stand)
    -. -bv cnj. cnjbv
    -bim -bnt cnjbim cnjbnt
    -bn -zn cnjbn cnjzn
    Important - there will be some verbs whose last consonant of the stem will end in a letter that will have to be replaced with another letter.
    * In a first conjugation verb all of the forms will normally change.
    * In a second conjugation verb only the first person singular will change.
