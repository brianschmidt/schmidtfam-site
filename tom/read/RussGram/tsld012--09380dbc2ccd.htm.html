    Adjectives
    In Russian you will find that adjectives can modify nouns. The adjectives have to be declined. Each adjective is declined according to its gender.
    Case Masculine Feminine Neuter
    Nom. Yjdsq Yjdfz Yjdjt
    Gen. Yjdjuj Yjdjq Yjdjuj
    Dat. Yjdjve Yjdjq Yjdjve
    Acc. Yjdsq/juj Yjde. Yjdjt
    Instr. Yjdsv Yjdjq Yjdsv
    Prep. Yjdjv Yjdjq Yjdjv
    Yjdsq=New
