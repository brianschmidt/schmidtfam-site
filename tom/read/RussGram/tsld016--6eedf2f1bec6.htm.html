    Verbs
    Russian verbs have a stem and an ending that can be conjugated. There are first and second conjugation verbs. First conjugation verbs end in -fnm- -znm and -nb
    If endings are not stressed your endings will be: Pyfnm (to know)
    -e or -. -tv pyf. pyftv
    -tim -tnt pyftim pyftnt
    -tn -en or -.n pyftn pyf.n
    If endings are stressed your endings will be: :bnm (to live)
    - -/v ;bde ;bd/v
    -/im -/nt ;bd/im ;bd/nt
    -/n - ;bd/n ;bden
    * If stems end in vowels spell the endings . for the first person singular and .n for the third person plural.
