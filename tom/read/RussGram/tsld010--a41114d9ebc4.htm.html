    Spelling Rules
    Do not panic! They are not as difficult as they may seem. Professor Thomas Beyer from Middlebury College has the 8-7-5 spelling rule method.
    1. The 8 Rule: after U- R- {- I- O- :- X- W you cannot write a > or Z. You write a E to replace the > and an F to replace the the Z.
    2. The 7 Rule: after U- R- {- I- O- :- X you cannot write an S. You write an B to replace the S.
    3. The 5 Rule: after I- O- :- X- W you can only write an J if it is accented and a T if it is not accented.
