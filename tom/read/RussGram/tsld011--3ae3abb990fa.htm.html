    Nouns
    Russian nouns name persons, places and things. However, when you use nouns in the Russian language, you have to know with which case you are using them. As a result, the nouns will have different endings depending on the case you are using. The table below shows how the endings of nouns will change with each case.
    Case Masculine Feminine Neuter
    Nom: Lheu/Exbntkm Ufptnf/N/nz Vtcnj/Vjht
    Gen: Lheuf/z Ufptns/b Vtcnf/z
    Dat: Lheue/. Ufptns/t Vtcne/.
    Accus. Animate Accu=Gen Ufptne/. Same as
    Inanimate Accu=Nom Masculine
    Inst. Lheujv/tv Ufptnjq/tq Vtcnjv/tv
    Prep. Lheut Ufptnt Vtcnt
    Lheu=friend, Exbntkm=male teacher, Ufptnf=newspaper, N/nz=aunt, Vtcnj=place and
    Vjht=sea
