    Prepositions
    * Prepositions used with the genitive case:
    Jn (from), Bp (out of), <tp (without), Lkz (for), Gjckt (after)
    * Prepositions used with the dative case:
    R (towards someone of something), Gj (on top of something)
    * Prepositions used with the accusative case:
    D (only when motion is involved), Yf (on, in, to)
    * Prepositions used with the instrumental case:
    Gjl (under), Gthtl (in front of), Pf (behind), Yfl (over, above),
    Hzljv c (next to)
    * Prepositions used with the prepositional case:
    J (about) and D and Yf (when they indicate location)
