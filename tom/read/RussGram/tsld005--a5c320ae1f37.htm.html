    Gender of Russian Words
    Russian words can either be Masculine, Feminine or Neuter.
  • Masculine words end is consonants. However, some words that end in a soft sign ( M ) can also be masculine. Example: Exbntkm (male teacher)
  • Feminine words end in ( F and Z ), some end in a soft sign ( M ).
  • Neuter words end in ( J and T )
