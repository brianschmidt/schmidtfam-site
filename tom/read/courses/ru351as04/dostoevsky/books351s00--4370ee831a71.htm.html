You will need to have your own personal copy for annotation of the following works by F. M. Dostoevsky.

 I have ordered for all the novels except The Gambler the excellent transaltions by Richard Peaver and Larissa Volokhonsky. Electronic texts of some novels are also available.

You may purchase the texts from the college bookstore, or less expensively using the internet. The best selection was offered by www.amazon.com

  • The Gambler
  • Notes From the Underground
  • Crime and Punishment [Electronic text]
  • The Idiot
  • Demons
  • The Adolescent
  •  
  • The Brothers Karamazov [Electronic text]
