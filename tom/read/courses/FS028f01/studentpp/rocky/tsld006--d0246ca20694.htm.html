About Rocky

  • Rocky Balboa is not the only hero of Rocky.

  • Sylvester Stallone was on the verge of destitution when he wrote a story loosely based upon a little-known, working-class underdog and New Jersey boxer Chuck Wepner, who challenged Muhammad Ali in a heavyweight title bout in early 1975 in Cleveland, Ohio.

  • MGM Pictures finally accepted the idea; only they were hesitant to cast Stallone as the leading role. MGM pursued Ryan ONeal, academy award nominee for his role in Love Story six years earlier, but he declined.

  • Stallone, who was nominated for his only academy award in this film, brilliantly portrays the character Rocky Balboa.
