Rocky (1976)

    The movie Rocky, a MGM picture, released in 1976 starring Sylvester Stallone was nominated for four academy awards (Best Actor, Best Actress, Best Supporting Actor, and Best Picture), winning one: Best Picture. Rocky is the film that inspired a nation (New York Post) and in doing so created a fictional hero: Rocky Balboa
