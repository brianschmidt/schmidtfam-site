Types of Heroes

Types of Heroes

  • fictional and non-fictional

  • war heroes, political heroes, athletic heroes, legendary heroes or ordinary heroes.

  • An ordinary hero is one who becomes a hero by some feat he or she has accomplished that the general public sees as heroic.

  • Rocky Balboa is a fictional hero who is combination of an ordinary hero, an athletic hero and a legendary hero.
