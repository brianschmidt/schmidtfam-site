Why Rocky is my hero

Why Rocky is my hero

  • Rocky Balboa is a hero to me because he stays steadfast in pursuit of his dreams.

  • I admire all people, not just athletes who set out a goal and then achieve it.

  • He is the common man who simply out works and out wills his opponents and obstacles in his life.

  • Rocky is a man who was given an opportunity and made the most of it.
