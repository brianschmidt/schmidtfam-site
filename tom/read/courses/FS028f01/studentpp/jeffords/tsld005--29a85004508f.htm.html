Jeffords leaving Republican Party

  • Unhappy and disagreeing with the contemporary statutes of the Republican party.

  • In particular, Jim disagreed with the Republican budget proposal, and overall stance on the environment.

  • On May 24, 2001, Jim left the Republican Party, becoming the only Independent Senator.

  • Jim is Independent by title, but votes with the Democrats.
