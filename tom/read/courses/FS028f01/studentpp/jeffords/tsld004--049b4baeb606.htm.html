Northeast Interstate Dairy Compact (NIDC)

Northeast Interstate Dairy Compact (NIDC)

  • Sets a price dairy farmers in the Northeast are paid by milk processors for their milk.

  • As a part of the NIDC there exists a compact commission, which has the authority to raise the amount paid above the federal minimum.

  • Vermonters believe the NIDC is the only thing keeping small family dairy farms alive in Vermont.
