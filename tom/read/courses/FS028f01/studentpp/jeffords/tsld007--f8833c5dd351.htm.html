Does Senator Jeffords Hold these?

  • He is courageous in many congressional decisions. Goes against the normal.

  • He displays leadership in government. As a Senior Senator he is very influential leader

  • He is compassionate to the needs of Vermonters and others nation wide.

  • He is a role model to all those who wish to go into politics.

  • Inspirational in his many controversial decisions.
