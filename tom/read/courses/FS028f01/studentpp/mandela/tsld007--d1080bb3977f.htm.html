Why Nelson Mandela Is My Hero

Why Nelson Mandela Is My Hero

    Mandela holds every intrinsic heroic trait:

  • He is a virtuous person and a good role model to those with bleak futures in their midst.

  • His determination is an example of inspiration to everyone.

  • His wife, Winnie, indicates his constant selflessness: If you became involved in our cause as he was, it was just not possible to think in terms of yourself at all.

  • His achievements are truly extraordinary.
