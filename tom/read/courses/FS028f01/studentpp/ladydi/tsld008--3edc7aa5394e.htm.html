I will fight for my children on any level so they can reach their potential as human beings and in their public duties.... Princess Diana

  • Because Diana is such a realistic woman, she inspires other women to live up to their potential. She cares for all children, no matter what. She is like a universal mother. Her love for children was evident in her social work and also in her own family life. Diana placed much value on the family unit. Princess Diana stresses how much she values what families think of her and how much she appreciates families. Motherhood is such a large part of who women are. Thus, Princess Dianas ability to be a good mother makes her a good heroine.
