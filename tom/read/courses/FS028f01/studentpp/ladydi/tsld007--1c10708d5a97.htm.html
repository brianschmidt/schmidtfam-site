Everyone needs to be valued. Everyone has the potential to give something back.... Princess Diana

  • Heroines are allowed to make mistakes too. Dianas adulterous marriage brought her down to the level of any other ordinary person. The Princess admitted to having her bouts with depression, self-mutilation, and bulimia. This allows the public, especially women to empathize with the way she was feeling. Dianas demonstration of how to overcome an obstacle and the way she turned a helpless life into one of aiding others shows why people look up to her and idealize her.
