Einstein Biographical Facts II

  • In 1896 he was able to enroll at the Swiss Federal Institute of Technology in Zurich.

  • By 1902 he had secured a position as patent examiner at the Swiss patent office in Bern, where he worked for the next 7 years.

  • The year 1905 was a significant year for science. At the age of 26, Einstein published four papers in the journal Annalen der Physik. Four Papers.
