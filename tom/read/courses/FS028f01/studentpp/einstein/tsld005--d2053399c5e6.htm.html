Einstein Biographical Facts I

Einstein Biographical Facts I

  • Born at Ulm, in Württemberg, Germany, on March 14, 1879.

  • When he was a boy, his family moved to Munich, which had a reputation for anti-Semitism.

  • No early signs of his future genius.

  • He was constantly the target of anti-Semitism by his classmates.

  • Anti-German vow and give up citizenship.
