Einstein Biographical Facts V

  • Einstein was a pacifist and an active leader in the international anti-war movement.

  • Advocated military preparedness against the threat of Nazism.

  • He was tireless in his efforts to prevent war.

  • He was urged by physicists to write his famous letter to President Roosevelt to make him aware of the inherent dangers of Germanys developing an atomic bomb.

  • When asked how World War III would be fought he said he didnt know. But he said World War IV would be fought with sticks and stones.
