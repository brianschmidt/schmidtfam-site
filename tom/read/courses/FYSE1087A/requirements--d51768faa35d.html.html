The Da Vinci Code

Fall Semester 2004

Course Requirements

Composition of grades

Course Requirements:

 

The aim of this course is to begin the "life of the mind," the work of a scholar, by examining Dan Brown's novel, The Da Vinci Code, that through its references to recorded and unrecorded history, to works of art and their "code," and to secret societies, has stimulated interest in a number of topics. The author himself challenges the reader by assering the "factual" nature of his novel. Our task is to make sense of the conflicting and contradictory sources of information.  We will read the novel as the starting point to identify questions, to gather and evaluate data, and to present convincing and scholarly defensible positions.

This is first and foremost a novel, a work of fiction. Literary scholarship has traditionally relied on a number of approaches to the including a close reading of the work itself, an examination of the historical context, comparison with other similar works and an examination of the author's life and works.

 

Composition of Grades:

I expect nothing less than full participation and excellent work all the time and on time. That level of performance deserves an "A." Anything short of the above will be graded accordingly.
