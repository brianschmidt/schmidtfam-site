Notes 46a

There are two sections numbered 46. I have numbered the second one as 46a.

lap for Latin "lono."the word actually means "bosom" Steiner uses the German word for "bosom."

Luna is both the Russian and the Latin for "moon." I have retained the form for poetic considerations. In fact Steiner uses the word "mond" and Russian has the word [mesjats] meaning "moon" or "month."

The third day, the day of Luna (the Moon), follows the days of Saturn and the Sun according to Rudolf Steiner's description in his Outline of Occult Knowledge.
