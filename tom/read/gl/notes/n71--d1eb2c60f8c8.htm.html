iamb, trochee, anapest, dactyl These traditional meters are also present in Russian poetry. Bely did extensive work on rhythm and metrics especially in his book Symbolism [Simvolizm] (1910). Steiner also addresses the meter of poetry in his descriptions of Eurythmy.

What are the gestures in eurythmy for "red" and "blue"?

cyan and crimson lie shades -- these are -- green, yellow, orange. Bely describes the progression and mixture of blue and yellow for green and yellow and red for orange.
