Step Six

Since the folder 'New Folder' is no longer needed, click on the 'New Folder' using the right mouse button and select 'Delete'.

On seeing the window , click 'Yes'.

Then click 'Yes to All' at the window .

Now the 'New Folder' has been successfully deleted.
