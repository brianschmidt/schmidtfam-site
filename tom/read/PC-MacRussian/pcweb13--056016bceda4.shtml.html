Step Three

Now the Russification of Netscape Navigator 3 is done. Yet everytime you go to a Russian page, the Russian will still be displayed like this:

Upon seeing this, click on the Options menu and go to Document Encoding. Then select Cyrillic(KOI8-R).

If, however, you still cannot read Russian, then click on the Options menu again and go to Document Encoding and try selecting Cyrillic(Win1251).
