Step Nine

Type in 'YaWERTY KOI8-R' in the box under 'Menu Name:'.

Then choose 'Russia' from the pull-down menu under 'Icon:'.

Finally select 'yawekoi8.wkb' from the pull-down menu under 'Keyboard File:'.

Click 'Save' button and click 'OK' on the window.

Then click 'Exit' button.

Now go to the next step.
